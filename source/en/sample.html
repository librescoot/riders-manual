Your keys

Hold an enrolled NFC keycard against the card symbol for about one second. You can also enroll the card from your starter kit.

Android NFC phone keys require an enabled build of Librescoot Mobile; they are hidden in release builds. Hold the enrolled, unlocked phone against the reader.

A teach-in card enrolls additional keys and never unlocks the scooter itself. Manage keys under [[Menu > Settings > Keycards]].

An enrolled keycard is all you need to ride. App, SIM card and cloud account are optional.

Chapter 3

Unlocking and riding

Three steps from a locked scooter to riding.

Safety

With the kickstand up, the scooter is ready to drive. Apply throttle only when seated securely and ready to move.

Before your first ride

  1. In park mode, test the blinkers, horn and seatbox switch. If any fail to respond, the front slot may be missing a battery.
  2. Ride the first few meters at walking pace in a quiet area and test both brakes. They should slow the scooter smoothly and predictably.

If the display shows code E14, the throttle was held at power-up. Release it and the message clears on its own.

Chapter 3  Unlocking and riding

On the road

Your speed appears in the center. Below it, the energy gauge shows current motor power.

Accelerating moves the bar right toward DISCHARGE. Coasting or braking moves it left toward REGEN, feeding energy back into the battery.

With a GPS fix, the road name and, when available, the speed limit also appear below the speedometer.

Braking and signaling

The right lever operates the front brake, the left lever the rear brake. Use the blinker switch on the left to signal; the display shows a green arrow {{icon:turn-left}} {{icon:turn-right}}. In drive mode, two short presses on the seatbox switch toggle the hazard lights on and off.

Shortcut menu while riding

Hold the seatbox switch until the entry you want is highlighted, release it, then press once more within three seconds. Use it to switch between cluster and map views or start a saved destination.

Safety

Keep your eyes on the road while riding. The main menu only opens in park mode; change settings while stopped.

Chapter 4

Parking and locking

Park, lock the scooter and engage the handlebar lock.

Check before walking away

Gently turn the handlebar left and right. If it moves freely, the handlebar lock did not engage: unlock the scooter, lock it again, and push the handlebar left within the window. Even though the scooter enters standby on its own after about 15 minutes, lock it yourself and check the handlebar lock.

Seatbox and alarm

In park mode, the seatbox switch opens the seatbox. It holds a helmet and gives access to the batteries. The scooter will not enter drive mode while the seatbox is open.

Enable the alarm under [[Menu > Settings > Vehicle > Alarm]]. It arms in standby; leave the scooter undisturbed after locking.

Chapter 4  Parking and locking

The four states of your scooter

The state controls whether you can ride and how much power is used. Locking switches from park mode to standby; unlocking switches back.

Manual hibernation

  1. Scooter in park mode, seatbox closed.
  2. Pull and hold both brake levers. After 3 s the version box appears; keep holding.
  3. After 15 s total, the display prompts to confirm. Tap a keycard or hold 15 s longer.
  4. Three seconds later, the scooter enters hibernation.

Releasing before the prompt cancels; afterwards, the seatbox switch cancels. Wake by holding both brake levers about 6 s or connecting over paired Bluetooth. A keycard alone will not wake it.

Backup batteries still discharge slowly in hibernation. Leave a charged main battery in front and check every few weeks.

Hop-on / hop-off for short stops

Under [[Menu > Settings > Vehicle > Hop On / Hop Off…]], set a combo on the brakes, horn and seatbox switch. The scooter stays on with a lock screen until you re-enter it. Lock with your key when leaving it unattended.
