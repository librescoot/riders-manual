Troubleshooting

First checks for faults, error codes, diagnostics and emergency start.

The scooter does not respond

  1. Hold both brake levers for about six seconds. If the scooter was in hibernation, it wakes up and the keycard works normally afterwards.
  2. Check that a charged main battery is seated in the front slot.
  3. If that fails, contact a workshop with unu experience or ask in the Librescoot community.

A deeply discharged AUX battery can prevent the scooter from activating the main battery. See {{p:k2b}} for keys and {{p:k3}} for the handlebar lock.

Next steps

For temperature faults, let the battery return to normal temperature. For B11, charge it fully once. For B32 to B35, remove it, wait one minute and reinsert it. If the fault remains, have the battery checked.

For B5, B9 and B12 to B14

Stop using the battery and do not attempt a test charge. Have it inspected, especially after a short circuit.

Next steps

For E14, release the throttle completely and wait briefly; the message clears automatically. If E12 returns repeatedly, have the throttle inspected.

For overheating, stop and let the motor or controller cool down. If the fault repeats, visit a workshop.

For motor or electronics faults

Park the scooter safely. Motor, sensor and wiring faults usually require workshop service.

Doctor: system check

In park mode, open [[Menu > Info > Doctor]]. It reads diagnostics separately from the main computer (MDB) and display (DBC): Services, Board connection, Kernel, Updates, Faults and Storage. It changes no settings.

Tap left to scroll down, hold right to scroll up. Once finished, tap right for "Check again" or hold left to go back.

"Not checked" means that area could not be evaluated. Each run stops after at most 40 seconds or when leaving the page or park mode.

Fault history

[[Menu > Info > Faults]] lists active and cleared entries with source, code, timestamps and count. Cleared entries remain in the log.

Emergency start without display

If the display is dead or stuck on the connecting screen, you can still move the scooter a short distance.

  1. Raise the kickstand.
  2. Hold both brake levers and press the seatbox switch at the same time.
Short moves only

The scooter can accelerate immediately with no speedometer, warnings or error messages. Move it only a short distance on safe, level ground, then resolve the cause.

Getting further help

Describe the issue accurately and note the fault code and installed versions. A photo of the display or Doctor report helps.

Ask questions on the Librescoot community Discord. Report reproducible software bugs in the issue tracker. For hardware faults, contact a workshop with unu experience.
