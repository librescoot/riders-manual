Info menu, version box and notices

In park mode, [[Menu > Info]] covers Components, Connectivity, Batteries, Consumption and Maps, plus Faults, SMS, About & Licenses and the "Doctor" diagnostic check ({{p:doctor}}).

Quick version check

In park mode, hold both brake levers together for about three seconds. The version box lists firmware versions for the main computer (MDB), display computer (DBC), Bluetooth module (nRF) and motor controller (ECU), plus backup battery values and the serial number. It stays visible for four seconds after release, or closes if you open the menu or leave park mode.

Detailed readings appear under [[Menu > Info > Components]]. When a fault is active, "Faults" also appears directly in the main menu.

Reading and deleting SMS

Open [[Menu > Info > SMS]] and select a message. Tap left to scroll down, hold right to scroll up, hold left to go back. Tap right to open the delete confirmation; "Cancel" is selected first.

Optional SMS forwarding is configured in the web interface under Settings (Advanced mode). It is off by default and carrier charges may apply.

Notices and sound cues

Multiple notices cycle automatically and long text scrolls. Critical notices remain visible during connection issues and updates.

With an amplifier connected, the cluster can play audio cues for blinkers, navigation and notices. On most scooters, the amplifier is disconnected.
