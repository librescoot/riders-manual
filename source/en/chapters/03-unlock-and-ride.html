Chapter 3

Unlocking and riding

Three steps from a locked scooter to riding.

Safety

With the kickstand up, the scooter is ready to drive. Apply throttle only when seated securely and ready to move.

Before your first ride

  1. In park mode, test the blinkers, horn and seatbox switch. If any fail to respond, the front slot may be missing a battery.
  2. Ride the first few meters at walking pace in a quiet area and test both brakes. They should slow the scooter smoothly and predictably.

If the display shows code E14, the throttle was held at power-up. Release it and the message clears on its own.

On the road

Your speed appears in the center. Below it, the energy gauge shows current motor power.

Accelerating moves the bar right toward DISCHARGE. Coasting or braking moves it left toward REGEN, feeding energy back into the battery.

With a GPS fix, the road name and, when available, the speed limit also appear below the speedometer.

Braking and signaling

The right lever operates the front brake, the left lever the rear brake. Use the blinker switch on the left to signal; the display shows a green arrow {{icon:turn-left}} {{icon:turn-right}}. In drive mode, two short presses on the seatbox switch toggle the hazard lights on and off.

Shortcut menu while riding

Hold the seatbox switch until the entry you want is highlighted, release it, then press once more within three seconds. Use it to switch between cluster and map views or start a saved destination.

Safety

Keep your eyes on the road while riding. The main menu only opens in park mode; change settings while stopped.

Setting up the shortcut menu

The shortcut menu gives access to selected actions in drive mode via the seatbox switch. See {{p:schnellmenue}} for the control sequence.

Choosing items

Depending on setup, the menu can switch between cluster and map views, change the theme, or navigate to a saved place. Route overview and "Skip stop" can also be added.

Change the items and their order in the web interface: enable Advanced mode in Settings and search for "Shortcut Menu Items" ({{p:lsd}}).

Two frequent destinations

  1. In park mode, open [[Menu > Settings > Map & Navigation]] and select "Quick nav".
  2. Assign a saved place to Slot 1 or Slot 2, and optionally choose an icon such as Home {{icon:home}} or Work {{icon:work}}.
  3. Include those quick destinations in the shortcut menu.

In park mode, you can also toggle hazard lights under [[Menu > Toggle Hazard Lights]]. In drive mode, two short presses on the seatbox switch toggle them.

At an intermediate stop

With the shortcut menu closed, pressing the seatbox switch pauses the route at an intermediate stop. An open shortcut menu holds the countdown ({{p:stopps}}).
