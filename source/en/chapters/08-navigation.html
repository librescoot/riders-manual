Navigation and trip history

Pick a destination, follow the route and review completed rides.

Before your first navigation

Offline navigation requires two data packs for your region: offline display maps and routing data. If you chose a region in the Installer, both are usually already installed.

Check installed packs under [[Menu > Info > Maps]]. With both installed, navigation works without a SIM card or internet.

For built-in single-map updates, if a matching patch is available for map or routing data, only the changes download; otherwise the full package downloads.

Choosing a destination

  1. Stop and open the menu in park mode with two short pulls on the left brake lever.
  2. Select [[Menu > Navigation]]. Enter an address, or pick a saved location or recent destination.
  3. For an address, choose city, street and, if needed, house number, then confirm.
  4. Raise the kickstand. The scooter enters drive mode and opens the map with the route.
Safety

Enter destinations while parked. The main menu is disabled in drive mode. Watch the road while riding.

Waiting for GPS

Until a fix is ready, the display shows "Waiting for GPS fix". Acquisition can take longer after long idle periods or with a blocked sky view. If accuracy is still too low for routing, a notice explains the wait.

Reading the map

The map shows your position and the active route. The top strip displays the next maneuver with distance and instruction.

Top right shows remaining distance, estimated time left and arrival time. When two maneuvers follow closely, a smaller preview can show the one after.

On the road with the map

A small plate names the current street using German road-sign colors: blue for motorways, yellow for federal roads and white for minor streets. A speed limit appears when the routing data includes one.

The map switches between light and dark themes with ambient light. At roundabouts, the instruction shows the exit to take.

Road signs and traffic conditions always take priority over display guidance.

The notice "Map coverage is unavailable here" means the region's map pack is missing. Check installed packs under [[Menu > Info > Maps]].

Stopping navigation

Stop and select [[Menu > Navigation > Stop Navigation]] in park mode. The route and turn instructions disappear. Use the main menu entry to switch back to cluster view.

Multi-stop routes

Plan several stops in the order you want to visit them. The route stays saved across restarts.

Adding and ordering stops

  1. In park mode, open [[Menu > Navigation > Route]] and choose "Add stop".
  2. Add an address, saved location or recent destination. Repeat for further stops.
  3. Select a stop to move, delete or set it as next. You can also skip a stop.

At an intermediate stop

While the scooter stays ready to drive, a 30-second countdown runs at a reached intermediate stop before advancing to the next stop. Brake levers do not control this prompt.

With the shortcut menu closed, pressing the seatbox switch pauses the route. An open shortcut menu holds the countdown. Leaving drive mode cancels the countdown; after a reached stop, entering drive mode again advances to the next stop.

To continue a paused route, stop and select [[Menu > Navigation > Route > Resume route]].

Regional offline maps

In regional mode, several map and routing packs can live on the scooter. The cluster selects a region from GPS position. Start and finish must lie in the active routing region; routes across separate regions are unsupported. Update regional packs over USB.

Checking map updates

Select "Check for Updates". "Maps are up to date" appears only after a successful check. Errors, uncheckable maps and busy transfers each show their own notice.

An update-available notice appears for at most five seconds and clears when you set off. It stays hidden in drive mode while remaining available in the menu.

Frequent destinations

Under [[Menu > Settings > Map & Navigation]], open "Quick nav" to assign two saved places for the shortcut menu ({{p:schnellmenue-einrichten}}).

Trip history

Open [[Menu > Trip history]] to browse completed rides. Select a ride for distance, ready-to-drive time, average speed and, when available, GPS top speed.

The map frames the recorded trace with start {{icon:trip-start}} and end {{icon:trip-end}}. Rides without points show "No GPS trace". Traces can contain gaps.

Retention

Only rides of 100 m or more are stored in history. Limit retention by age, trip count or storage size, or turn automatic cleanup off, under Trip counter in Vehicle settings.

In trip details, hold the left brake to return to the list. Supported apps can retrieve completed trips and stored GPS points.
