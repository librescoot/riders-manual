Chapter 1  Getting to know your scooter

Under the seatbox and in the footwell

In park mode, open the seatbox with the seatbox switch on the left handlebar. Underneath are the battery slots and storage space.

Front and rear battery slots

With standard factory wiring, the battery in the front slot powers the scooter. The rear slot holds a spare battery. See {{p:k7}} for swapping batteries.

While the seatbox is open, the status bar shows {{icon:seat-open}} and the scooter will not enter drive mode. Close the seatbox before riding.

Under the footboard

Under the screwed-down footwell cover sits the scooter's main computer, the MDB. You can connect a computer to its USB port to transfer files or open the web interface.

In daily use, the cover stays closed. Follow the steps on {{p:usb}} when opening and closing it.
